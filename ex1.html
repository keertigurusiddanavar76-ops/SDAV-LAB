Binomial Probability Formula

P(X = k) = C(n, k) × pk × (1 − p)n−k

The cumulative probability P(X ≤ 10) is the sum of P(X = k) for all values of k from 0 through 10.

Complementary Probability

P(X > 10) = 1 − P(X ≤ 10)

P(X > 10) = 0.213099 (21.3099%)

4. Probability Mass Function (PMF)

The graph shows the probability of each possible number of successes. The green bar identifies the highest probability, and the red dashed line indicates the mean.

5. Inference from the Visualization

For n = 15 and p = 0.6, the mean is 9 and the variance is 3.6. The highest probability occurs at 9 successes. The PMF graph peaks around the mean and decreases toward both extremes, showing that outcomes near 9 successes are more likely than outcomes near 0 or 15.
