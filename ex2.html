Exercise 2 – Poisson Distribution

An AI-based monitoring system has an average event rate of λ = 6. Calculate probabilities and compare λ = 2, 6, and 10.

Poisson Formula:

P(X = k) = e−λ λk / k!

For a Poisson distribution, mean = variance = λ.

As λ increases from 2 to 6 to 10, the distribution shifts to the right. The mean and variance increase together. The distribution becomes wider in absolute terms and generally more symmetric for larger values of λ.
