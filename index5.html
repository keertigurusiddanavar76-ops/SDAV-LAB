Probability Mass Function (PMF)

The PMF calculates the probability of obtaining exactly k successes.

Mathematical Expression
P(X = k) = C(n, k) pk (1 − p)(n − k)

Probability of Exactly 7 Successes

0.1844
18.44%
This is the probability of obtaining exactly 7 successes in 20 trials.

Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF)

The CDF calculates the probability of k or fewer successes.

Cumulative Probability
P(X ≤ k) = ∑x = 0k C(n, x) px (1 − p)(n − x)
Complementary Probability
P(X > k) = 1 − P(X ≤ k)
